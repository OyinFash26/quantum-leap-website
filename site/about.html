About us

Trusted facility managers since 2012

An integrated facility management, real estate and property solutions company helping businesses, developers, institutions and property owners keep their properties running smoothly.

Who we are

One trusted partner for the care of your property

Quantum Leap Management Services Ltd provides a full range of facility management services that protect property value, improve operational efficiency, and make buildings better for the people who use them, through proactive maintenance and excellent service.

Our strength is offering several related services under one trusted brand, so clients work with a single partner instead of multiple vendors. From light building cleaning to strategic property management, we leave your environment clean, safe and productive.

Whether it's a residential estate, commercial building, school, healthcare facility or corporate office, we tailor our solutions to each client's needs.

Our approach

We stand on the four pillars of facility management

A building only runs well when all four work together. We plan every contract around them.

People

Trained, supervised staff who know your property and the people who use it.

Process

Clear schedules, checklists and reporting, so nothing depends on memory.

Building

The structure, systems and equipment, kept in good working order.

Technology

Smart tools to track maintenance, log issues and respond faster.
