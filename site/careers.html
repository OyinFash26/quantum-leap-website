Facility & Operations Officer

Full-timeEntry-levelAjah/Ogombo axis, Lagos

Support the day-to-day running of the properties we manage in the Ajah/Ogombo area, working with our field teams, clients and service providers, and help grow our client base.

What you'll do

  • Support the day-to-day supervision, inspection and maintenance of properties and facility operations.
  • Coordinate and follow up with cleaners, artisans, technicians and other service providers.
  • Prepare weekly and monthly operational reports and maintain property records.
  • Track maintenance issues, assigned tasks and outstanding actions.
  • Assist with client communication, inquiries, complaints and service follow-up.
  • Identify potential clients and properties within the target areas.
  • Conduct physical and online marketing activities to generate new leads.

What we're looking for

  • OND or HND in any relevant discipline.
  • 0–2 years of experience.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Basic marketing and communication skills.
  • Good organisational, administrative and record-keeping skills.
  • Must be proactive, responsible, teachable and willing to work in the field.
  • Must reside within or be able to easily commute to the Ajah/Ogombo axis.
  • Previous experience is not mandatory, but internship, NYSC, sales, customer service, property or facility management experience is an advantage.
